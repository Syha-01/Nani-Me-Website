Since MyAnimeList and AniList

What changed from 2.0 to 4.0.

2.015 September
  • Your list on MyAnimeList: what you watch here is written there as you go, with your score at the end.
  • When the usual site has no stream, Tsuzuku tries two more on its own. Downloads too.
  • Every season in order, related shows, the cast and studio, and real episode names.
  • The MyAnimeList discussion for each episode, under the player.
AniList19 to 20 September
  • AniList sync, the same way as MyAnimeList, with series pages following the site you read.
  • Importing a long list no longer holds the screen. Shows appear in Saved as they are found.
  • A first screen asking whose list this is: MyAnimeList, AniList or none.
  • Schedule rebuilt around your shows, with a reminder ten minutes before each episode.
3.024 September
  • Fit, zoom, stretch, 16:9 or 4:3, and drag across the picture to move through the episode.
  • Sub and dub switch from inside the player and stay on the same episode.
  • Series pages open over what you tapped, with your status and score under Play.
  • Skip intros and outros on their own, and smoother scrolling throughout.
3.128 to 30 September
  • Downloads keep going with the screen off. Queue a season before bed.
  • Picture-in-picture, subtitles included.
  • Soft dark and Soft light themes, and subtitles you can move up or down.
  • Hide ecchi, to leave it out of Home, Search, Browse and Schedule.
Updates3 to 7 October
  • A new player: buttons that say what they do, and every setting in one sheet.
  • Pinch to zoom from 25% to 300%, and genres as coloured tiles in Search.
  • Wallpapers from every season on a series page, and your list status on every poster.
  • Closing picture-in-picture stops the episode.
3.26 October
  • Saved episodes play in any player, with the subtitles inside the file.
  • Save all, to write every saved episode to a folder at once.
  • Sharing sends a normal video named after the show and episode.
4.010 October
  • Make Tsuzuku yours: a setup with recommendations for your internet.
  • KickAssAnime, and downloads down to 360p.
  • Your own accent colour, a preferred quality you can set in Settings, and a download source of its own.
  • Previous and next in the player, openings skipped properly, and seek previews.

Make Tsuzuku yours

A short setup when you first open 4.0.

Based on your internet

Tell it how fast your internet is. It recommends a streaming quality and download size. You can change them.

Keeps your settings

Anything you already changed stays as it is.

Run it again any time

It is in Settings › Set up Tsuzuku.

Subtitles

Style, size, colour, font and position, with a live preview.

Accent colour

Six colours for buttons and progress bars. Also in Settings › Theme.

Player layout

Mini screen, or directly to full screen.

Smaller downloads

For slow internet and limited data.

New source: KickAssAnime

Has 720p and 360p on most shows.

About a third of the size

360p is about 130 MB an episode. 1080p is about 350 MB.

Faster downloads

6 parts at once instead of 3. Lower it in Settings › Downloads if downloads fail.

Skips work offline

Saved episodes skip openings and endings too.

Sound in sync

Saved videos no longer have the sound slightly early.

Finds another source

If a source does not have a show, it switches to one that does.

The player

Easier to use.

Previous and next episode

Next to the 10-second skips in full screen.

Better skipping

Openings at the very start now skip. Openings and endings are found for more episodes.

Seek previews

See a picture while you drag the timeline. Turn on in Settings › Playback.

New to Tsuzuku?

What it does.

Watch

  • Subbed or dubbed, in the quality you pick.
  • Picks up where you left off, on every show.
  • Picture-in-picture and pinch to zoom.

Offline

  • Download a whole season, even with the screen off.
  • Saved episodes play with no internet, subtitles and skips included.
  • Save them to a folder or share them as normal videos.

Your list

  • MyAnimeList or AniList, kept up to date as you watch.
  • Or no account at all.
  • A schedule of your shows, with reminders.

Subtitles

  • Six fonts, or your own.
  • Translated into Spanish, Japanese or Italian on your phone.
  • Size, colour, style and where they sit.

Your way

  • Four themes and any accent colour.
  • Wallpapers from the shows you watch.
  • Hide ecchi with one switch.

Free

  • No tiers and nothing kept for a paid version.
  • Fixes arrive on their own when you open the app.
  • A Discord where bugs get found and fixed.
