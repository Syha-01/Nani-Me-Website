Privacy

Last updated 14 November 2026

Tsuzuku is a free Android app, made by one developer. You can use it without an account. This page lists everything it stores about you, why, and how to have it deleted.

On your phone only

Your library, watch progress, downloads and settings live on your phone. They don't leave it unless you turn on one of the features below.

If you join the rankings or make an account

  • Your username and, if you set them, a password (stored only as a hash, never readable) and an email address. The email is used only to help you back into your account, for example to send a password reset code.
  • What you watch for the rankings: the show, the episode and the minutes watched, which make up the leaderboards and your monthly wrap.
  • Your profile: your picture, the items you own or wear from the shop, your coins, who you follow, and your stories.
  • Chat: the messages you send in chats, watch parties and direct messages.
  • A backup of your library, if you turn backup on, so you can restore it on another phone.
  • MyAnimeList or AniList: if you sign in with one, Tsuzuku keeps your user name and id there, and uses their sign-in to update your list on your behalf. Your password for those sites never reaches Tsuzuku.
  • Ko-fi supporters: the support and a scrambled copy (a hash) of your Ko-fi email, only to recognise renewals.

Everyone who uses the app

  • A random install id and the app version, sent at most once a day, so I know how many people use the app. It isn't tied to your name or your phone's identity.
  • Playback problems: when an episode fails to load, the show, episode and the server that failed, so broken sources can be fixed. No personal details are included.
  • Contact the developer: anything you send there, with your app version and phone model, and a way to reach you if you give one. Bug reports and feature requests are shown to other users and copied to the Tsuzuku Discord; "Something else" is seen only by you and me.

What Tsuzuku doesn't do

  • No ads and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics or crash reporting services.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone for marketing.

Where it's kept

Account data is stored on Convex, the service that runs Tsuzuku's server. Password reset emails are sent from tsuzuku.support@gmail.com through Gmail. Videos come from third-party sites, which see your connection as they would in a browser.

Deleting your data

To have your account and everything tied to it deleted, send a request from Settings › Contact the developer, or email tsuzuku.support@gmail.com with your username. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone.
